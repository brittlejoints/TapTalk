## What Tap Talk is

Tap Talk is a free communication aid. You tap words and phrases, and your device speaks them. It is meant to help, not to replace professional advice from a speech and language therapist, doctor or other expert.

## Tap Talk is not an emergency service

This matters most, so it comes first.

- **The Help screen does not call anyone.** It speaks phrases out loud and can open your Messages app with an alert ready. **Nothing is sent until you tap Send there.**
- A message may not be delivered. Your phone, your signal, your contacts' phones and your phone company all affect that, and Tap Talk cannot check any of them.
- A location in an alert may be missing or wrong.
- **In an emergency, call your local emergency number.** Do not rely on Tap Talk alone.

## Not a medical device

Tap Talk is not a medical device and makes no medical claims. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor anything.

## Things that may not work

Tap Talk depends on your browser and device. Voices differ between devices, and a voice or language may not be available on yours. Speech, location, photos and installing to the home screen work differently across browsers. Updates may change how the app looks or works. Please do not rely on Tap Talk as your only way to communicate. Keep a backup way to be understood.
