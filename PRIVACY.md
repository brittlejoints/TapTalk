# Privacy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tap Talk is an app people use to speak by tapping words. What someone says and sets up in it is personal, so the short version is this: **Tap Talk does not collect your data. Everything you add stays on your own device.**

## What Tap Talk does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
- No data sent to us or to any server we run. Tap Talk has no server and makes no network requests while you use it.
- No fonts or icons loaded from other websites. They are part of the app, so it works offline.

## What is saved on your device

Tap Talk saves the following in your browser's own storage, on your device only:

- Your settings: language, text size, voice and speed, your name, and the on or off choices.
- Your custom tiles: the words, colors, icons or emoji you pick, and any photos you add (photos are shrunk on your device before they are saved).
- Your saved phrases.
- Your emergency contacts: names, phone numbers and how you know them.
- Two small markers: that you have finished the welcome guide, and the date of the last greeting.

This information is **not encrypted**. Anyone who can use your unlocked device and browser could see it. Do not store anything in Tap Talk that you would not want them to see. Tap Talk never reads this information except to show it back to you.

## Speech

Tap Talk uses the text-to-speech voices that come with your browser and device. On most phones and tablets these run on the device. Some browsers also offer online voices, which send the words to be spoken to the company that runs that voice. Tap Talk does not choose or control this. If privacy matters, pick a voice that is installed on your device in Settings.

## Location and emergency alerts

The Help screen can open your Messages app with an alert ready for your emergency contacts. If you turn on "Include my location", your device is asked for its location once, to add a map link to the text of the message. Tap Talk does not save your location or send it anywhere itself. **You choose whether to send the message by tapping Send in your Messages app**, and it then travels through your phone and phone company like any other text message.

## Backup files

The backup button in Settings creates a file with everything listed above, including phone numbers. The file is plain text and is yours. Keep it somewhere private, and delete it when you no longer need it. Tap Talk does not upload it anywhere.

## Links in the app

The About section in Settings has links to this page, the terms, the license and the source code on GitHub. They open in your browser only when you tap them. Tap Talk itself makes no request when you do.

## Where the app is served from

Tap Talk is published on GitHub Pages. When you open the site, GitHub may keep standard records such as your IP address, as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Tap Talk does not receive or use those records. After the first visit the app is stored on your device and opens without a connection.

## Children and caregivers

Tap Talk collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Parents and caregivers who set it up for someone else should decide what to save, such as photos and contacts, and keep any backup file private.

## Deleting your data

- Remove individual items in Settings: custom tiles, saved phrases and emergency contacts.
- Remove everything by clearing the site's data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app from your home screen and clearing its data.
- Delete any backup file you made.

Because the data is only on your device, we cannot see it or delete it for you.

## Changes

If Tap Talk ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top. The history of changes is in the project's repository.
