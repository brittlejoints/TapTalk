# Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Tap Talk is an app people use to speak by tapping words. What someone says and sets up in it is personal, so the short version is this: **Tap Talk does not collect your data. Everything you add stays on your own device.** The only exception is optional: if you switch on "Help improve Tap Talk" in Settings, the app sends anonymous weekly counts (never your words), described below. It is off unless you turn it on.

## What Tap Talk does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No tracking, advertising or cookies, and no analytics unless you switch on "Help improve Tap Talk".
- Unless you switch that on, no data is sent to us or to any server, and Tap Talk makes no network requests while you use it.
- No fonts or icons loaded from other websites. They are part of the app, so it works offline.

## What is saved on your device

Tap Talk saves the following in your browser's own storage, on your device only:

- Your settings: language, text size, voice and speed, your name, and the on or off choices.
- Your custom tiles: the words, colors, icons or emoji you pick, and any photos you add (photos are shrunk on your device before they are saved).
- Your saved phrases.
- Your emergency contacts: names, phone numbers and how you know them.
- A few small markers: that you have finished the welcome guide, the date of the last greeting, the week you first opened Tap Talk, and the short tag of the link that first opened it (for example `?ref=newsletter`), if it had one. The last two leave your device only if you switch on "Help improve Tap Talk".
- If you switch on "Help improve Tap Talk": this week's counts, until they are sent.

This information is **not encrypted**. Anyone who can use your unlocked device and browser could see it. Do not store anything in Tap Talk that you would not want them to see. Tap Talk never reads this information except to show it back to you.

## Speech

Tap Talk uses the text-to-speech voices that come with your browser and device. On most phones and tablets these run on the device. Some browsers also offer online voices, which send the words to be spoken to the company that runs that voice. Tap Talk does not choose or control this. If privacy matters, pick a voice that is installed on your device in Settings.

## Location and emergency alerts

The Help screen can open your Messages app with an alert ready for your emergency contacts. If you turn on "Include my location", your device is asked for its location once, to add a map link to the text of the message. Tap Talk does not save your location or send it anywhere itself. **You choose whether to send the message by tapping Send in your Messages app**, and it then travels through your phone and phone company like any other text message.

## Backup files

The backup button in Settings creates a file with everything listed above, including phone numbers. The file is plain text and is yours. Keep it somewhere private, and delete it when you no longer need it. Tap Talk does not upload it anywhere.

## Optional anonymous usage counts ("Help improve Tap Talk")

This is **off by default**. If you switch it on in Settings, once a week, after the week ends, Tap Talk sends one small report about that week, only when your device is online. Settings shows exactly what the report holds ("See exactly what is shared").

A report contains only:

- **Counts for the week:** times the app was opened, tiles tapped, messages spoken, typed entries, phrases saved, times Help was opened, Help phrases spoken, emergency alerts started, and tiles added.
- **Coarse facts:** the week (its Monday), the app version, language, text size, whether the device is a phone, tablet or computer, whether Tap Talk is installed on the home screen, how many weeks Tap Talk has been used on this device, roughly how many of your own tiles you have (none, 1 to 5, 6 to 20, or more), whether any emergency contacts are set up (not who), and whether high visibility, speak each tile and playful touches are on.
- **The link tag**, if the link that first opened Tap Talk had one (letters, numbers and dashes only).

A report **never** contains your words, messages or phrases, tile names or pictures, your name, contacts or phone numbers, your location, the time of day you used the app, or any identifier for you or your device. Reports cannot be linked to each other or to a person.

Reports go to a database run for Tap Talk on Supabase (Supabase, Inc.). Like any web service, Supabase may keep standard request records such as IP addresses, as described in [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy); Tap Talk does not store IP addresses with reports or use those records. Reports are used only to understand how Tap Talk is used, to keep it running and to improve and promote it. They are never sold or used for advertising, and they are deleted after 24 months.

Turning the switch off stops all reports and deletes the counts kept on your device. Because reports carry no identifier, ones already sent cannot be found or removed individually.

## Links in the app

The About section in Settings has links to this page, the terms, the license and the source code on GitHub. They open in your browser only when you tap them. Tap Talk itself makes no request when you do.

## Where the app is served from

Tap Talk is published on GitHub Pages. When you open the site, GitHub may keep standard records such as your IP address, as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Tap Talk does not receive or use those records. After the first visit the app is stored on your device and opens without a connection.

## Children and caregivers

Tap Talk collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The optional usage counts above are anonymous and contain no personal information; a caregiver can leave them off. Parents and caregivers who set it up for someone else should decide what to save, such as photos and contacts, and keep any backup file private.

## Deleting your data

- Remove individual items in Settings: custom tiles, saved phrases and emergency contacts.
- Remove everything by clearing the site's data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app from your home screen and clearing its data.
- Delete any backup file you made.

Because the data is only on your device, we cannot see it or delete it for you. Anonymous usage counts, if you shared them, cannot be traced back to you.

## Changes

If Tap Talk ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top. The history of changes is in the project's repository.
